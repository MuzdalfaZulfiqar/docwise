# Acme Employee Handbook

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote workers must be
reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Equipment such as monitors and chairs can be
expensed up to 500 dollars per year.

## Vacation and Leave

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 vacation days per month. Unused vacation days carry over up to a maximum
of 10 days. Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary
caregiver.

## Security Requirements

All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated only when
a compromise is suspected. Security incidents must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.

## Expenses

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Receipts are required for any expense above
25 dollars. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase.
